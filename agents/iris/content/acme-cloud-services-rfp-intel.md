## Executive summary

Federal demand for **cloud migration + cybersecurity support** under NAICS 541512 remains concentrated at **DoD, DHS, VA, and GSA**, with large integrators holding enterprise IT and cyber vehicles. For a Q3 2026 DoD-shaped opportunity, expect **full-and-open or best-value** competition, **JWCC-class cloud primitives** (AWS/Azure/GCP/Oracle) as underlying IaaS, and **systems integrator primes** for migration, zero trust, and managed security services.

## Competitive implications for ACME (capture hypotheses)

1. **Incumbent pressure:** If ACME is new to agency, assume **2–4 established SIs** with past performance on same agency cloud/cyber programs.  
2. **Discriminators to research next:** Zero trust implementation, IL5/IL6 experience, CMMC alignment, migration factory metrics, classified vs unclassified scope.  
3. **Pricing:** Obligation history on comparable NAICS 541512 task orders often more predictive than ceiling; pull 3–5 comparable awards from USAspending when RFP drops.  
4. **Vehicle strategy:** Confirm whether opp sits on **OASIS+, Alliant 2/3, JWCC task order, or agency BPA** — gates teaming and prime/sub role.
