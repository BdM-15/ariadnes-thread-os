## Executive summary

USACE NAU awarded a **stand-alone DBB firm-fixed-price** contract on **19 Feb 2026** to **PORR Government Services Poland Sp. z o.o.** (UEI HX7XQNM7N875) for **$101,075,586.23** (award **W912GB26CA001**) to construct the **Powidz Munitions Storage Area**: **56 earth-covered magazines (ECMs)**, maintenance/admin buildings, rail spur, paving, fencing, and utilities over **~1,020 calendar days** from NTP. The procurement is explicitly a **recompete of W912GB21R0008**, with market shaping under **W912GB24R0050** (sources sought / industry day). **Primary capture on this notice is closed**; value is **competitive intel**, **follow-on ammo/ECM work in Poland**, and **teaming posture** with the incumbent Polish prime.

## 5. Key compliance constraints (NATO-only NCB+)

From solicitation description (award notice / intake; validate against final RFP in PIEE):

| Constraint | Requirement | Capture implication |
|------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **NCB+ (nationality)** | Prime, subcontractors, manufacturers, and **material origin** → **NATO member nations only** (32 countries per intake). | U.S.-only primes **cannot prime** unless through **U.S. firm established in NATO nation** (per solicitation structure). |
| **Competition** | Full and open **within** NATO restriction; **SBA set-aside waived OCONUS**. | Small-business set-asides generally **not** applied; **large Polish/European** contractors competitive. |
| **Standards** | ECM/precast must meet **U.S. and European** standards (PORR press confirms dual compliance burden). | QA/QC and **ERDC/USACE** materials oversight likely ([ERDC/GSL Poland bunkers](https://americas-engineers.com/materials-engineers-help-ensure-usace-project-success-in-poland-americas-engineers/)). |
| **Site access** | Active **Polish military / U.S.** operating base — security, escort, and logistics constraints. | Local presence and **Polish workforce** differentiation. |
| **Contract type** | **DBB FFP** — contractor bears **design-bid-build** risk on **100% design** basis. | Need **ECM design-build** past performance; bonding/financing at **~$100M** scale. |
| **SAM / PIEE** | Registration and **ProjNet** Q&A (key MZ69H3-4SQI9I per intake). | Foreign entities need **SAM.gov** + PIEE access for USACE Europe. |
