## 2. EAGLE vehicle & Fort Benning lineage

### 2.1 Program mechanics

**Enhanced Army Global Logistics Enterprise (EAGLE)** uses **Basic Ordering Agreements (BOAs)** under **ACC-RI** so the Army can add qualified holders and compete **installation task orders** for standardized **maintenance, supply, and transportation** PWS ([GovExec EAGLE brief](https://cdn.govexec.com/media/gbc/docs/eaglecntrct_final_rev3.pdf); [Army EAGLE contractor selection, 2012](https://www.army.mil/article/87229/asc_selects_eagle_contractors)).

- **EAGLE II wave:** multi-award BOAs **July 2017**, ordering through **July 2027**; primary NAICS **561210**, PSC **J025** ([HigherGov EAGLE II vehicle](https://www.highergov.com/vehicle/enhanced-army-global-logistics-enterprise-ii-eagle-ii-4097/)).
- **FY25 BOA refresh:** **W519TC-25-R-0044** (Jun 2025) adds **small-business** BOA holders — Benning TO remains **full & open** ([GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/army-fy-2025-eagle-ii-logistics-support-program-solicitation)).
- **Apr 2026:** ACC-RI **Mobile Training Team** rollout streamlines contract administration across **nine EAGLE locations** ([Army.mil](https://www.army.mil/article/291492/army_streamlines_contract_support_for_eagle_sites)).
