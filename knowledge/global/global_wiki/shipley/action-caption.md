> **Entity type:** `concept`

Shipley graphics convention: every figure has a Figure Number, a Figure TITLE that asserts a BENEFIT (not just describes the image), and a Caption that delivers the PROOF. Example from Shipley model doc (p. 315): Figure 2.2-1, title 'Superior Glide Ratio', caption 'An 8:1 glide ratio gives the UQ601 longer unpowered range than other commercially available ultralights.' Evaluators skim figures first — action captions let a graphic sell even when the body text isn't read. Anti-pattern: neutral titles like 'System Architecture Diagram' that miss the persuasive moment.
