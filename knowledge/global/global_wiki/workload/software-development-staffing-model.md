> **Entity type:** `concept`

Development staffing based on scope and methodology. Drivers: (1) Lines of code or function points (for new development), (2) Story points per sprint (Agile), (3) Defect rates and rework factors, (4) Team composition (dev:test:PM ratios). Typical Agile team: 5-9 members including developers, testers, Scrum Master, Product Owner. Velocity-based planning: historical story points per sprint × planned sprints = capacity. Rule of thumb: 1 tester per 3-4 developers. Add 10-15% for DevSecOps, CI/CD pipeline maintenance, and technical debt.
