# Pressure tier

> One atomic concept page (Karpathy LLM wiki). Hover the **info** icon in the app for the short tip; this page compounds context over time.

## What it means

How much of an agency’s spend is still on flexible pricing. High = buyer may face policy or oversight pressure to shift toward firm-fixed or performance-based deals. Use to prioritize shaping conversations, not as a guarantee.
