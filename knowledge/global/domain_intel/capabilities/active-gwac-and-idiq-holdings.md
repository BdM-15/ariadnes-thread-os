# Active GWAC and IDIQ Holdings

KBR maintains contract vehicles (GWACs, IDIQs, BPAs) that enable federal task-order access. This page documents **what is verifiable** from Tier-1 corporate sources and **what requires Tier-4** (SAM.gov, GSA schedules, contract registry exports) before proposal citation.

## Key signals + citations

| Signal | Citation |
|--------|----------|
| No authoritative public vehicle catalog on kbr.com marketing IA | Iris W4 §1 — Tier-1 gap |
| Careers posts reference SeaPort-NxG / OASIS+ **experience** (not award proof) | Iris Tier-2 signal |
| Named vehicle list (OASIS+, Alliant, SeaPort, ITES, SEWP, etc.) | **Not bid truth** until Tier-4 appendix |

## Offering

Honest **open inventory** posture: vehicles accelerate pursuit timing and constrain which RFPs are addressable — cite only registry-backed vehicle names and numbers after Iris/Hephaestus SAM/GSA appendix.

## Bid fit

Use when an RFP asks for contract vehicle eligibility or past IDIQ performance — until Tier-4 appendix exists, frame as **transition-risk / open inventory** in proposals; do not assert OASIS+ or SeaPort **awards** from careers JD text alone.

## Evidence

- Tier-1: none for full catalog (Iris rescout).
- Tier-2: careers JD signals only — **not** proof of award.
- Tier-4: SAM/GSA/contract appendix — **required** for named vehicles.

## Open questions
